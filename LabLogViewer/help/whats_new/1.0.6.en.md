### Sub-projects
- **Pick project folders level by level**: when the project chosen in the project list has sub-projects (Lab Control 1.0.10 saves into 數據集散地/project/sub-project/…), another list appears to its right with the sub-projects, and another one for each deeper level. The first entry "(only …)" shows the whole upper project including all its sub-projects. When there is no room the lists wrap to the next line instead of cutting the names.
