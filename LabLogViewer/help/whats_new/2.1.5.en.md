### Lab data on the NAS, smooth heatmaps, optional pets
- **Open Lab Data Folder (NAS / VPN)** (File menu, Ctrl+Shift+O): opens the lab's shared "數據集散地" — the lab network `\\192.168.50.2\ccuqel\數據集散地` first, then the VPN `\\100.114.33.20\ccuqel\數據集散地`. On a Mac it offers to mount the share first.
- **2D heatmaps are no longer pixelated**: with few sweeps (e.g. 77 × 10001 points) the picture is interpolated smoothly for display; readouts, cuts and exports still use the measured points. Turn it off with View > Smooth 2D Heatmap.
- **Pets are an optional download** (the installer is ~210 MB smaller): tick "pet pictures" in the Windows one-click setup, answer "y" in the Mac install, or download them later in Settings > Pet. Computers that already had a pet: the Windows update moves the pictures to the new place; on a Mac LabLogViewer offers to download them.
