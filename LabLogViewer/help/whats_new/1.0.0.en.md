### LabLogViewer 1.0.0 — official release
- **Official release**: LabLogViewer and the measurement program (Lab Control) are both **1.0.0**. Version numbers start again here (0.x – 2.1.6 were development versions); the features are those of 2.1.6.
- Computers that have a 2.x version **install 1.0.0 once by hand** (lab NAS download page or the one-click setup); later updates arrive as usual and install from inside the program. Data, tags, stars and comments are kept.
- Everything from 2.1.6 is included: NAS / VPN data copied locally before reading, big folders that fill as you scroll, shortcuts (Settings > Shortcuts), phase delay, drag-and-drop open, project folders, the Lab Control link.
- Notes of the earlier versions: `help/whats_new/legacy/`.
