### Lab Control .nc files (new)
- Lab Control 0.0.20+ saves measurements as xarray / NetCDF (`.nc`). LabLogViewer now opens them directly: 1D, 2D, N-D slicing, Marks, analysis and export work exactly as for Labber logs.
- **Open Database** (or "Open the whole project in the reader" from the Quantum Engineering Platform) lists `.nc` files next to `.hdf5` files.
- Sweep axes, units (e.g. mA), logged channels (e.g. complex VNA - S21), the frequency axis, tags, user, paper-library project, comment and instrument settings are read from the file; points that were not measured stay empty.
- The Open dialog has a new "Lab Control NetCDF (*.nc)" filter.
