### Big data, VPN, shortcuts and phase delay
- **Faster data on the NAS / over the VPN**: a file on a network drive is copied to this computer once (large sequential reads, much faster than many small ones); previews, Viewers, trace switching and plot changes then read the local copy. A copy is used only while it is identical to the original, and a file that is still being written is never copied. Opening a larger file shows the copy progress (Cancel works). Settings > General shows the space the copies take and clears them.
- **The preview keeps up when you switch Data**: the previous one stops reading instead of competing for the network.
- **No blank window when opening a Viewer**: the data is ready before the Viewer opens.
- **Big folders**: with more than 100 entries the first 100 are shown and more are added as you scroll; opening a folder on a network drive no longer opens every file. Dates and sweep dimensions are read in the background, **the folder you are looking at first**, and filled into the list as they arrive (no rebuild); background reading pauses while a preview or Viewer reads.
- **Smoother trace switching**: from the second trace on, the channel is read once and traces are switched in memory.
- **Default database folder**: a measurement computer opens its Desktop "數據集散地"; other computers open the one on the NAS.
- **Project folder**: choose a project above Folder to show that project's folder.
- **Drag to open**: drop data files (.hdf5, .h5, .nc) or a folder from Explorer / Finder onto any LabLogViewer window.
- **Shortcuts** (Viewer): Esc full view, M Point Mark on/off, 1 / 2 Plot Mode, F Maximize Plot, P data points, A auto color range (2D), H hide controls, D phase delay on/off, T Traces. Change or turn them off in Settings > Shortcuts.
- **Phase delay** (Plot config): removes the electrical delay τ of the line (z·exp(+i·2π·f·τ), f is the X axis, Hz / MHz / GHz taken into account). "Calculate" fits τ to the phase slope at both ends of the sweep.
- **With Lab Control**: a finished measurement opens in LabLogViewer automatically (except two-layer split files); dragging Data from LabLogViewer onto Lab Control's measurement window applies that file's settings.
- **Smaller indentation** of sub-folders in Folder.
- **In-app update**: "Update now" now also shows when the GitHub feed announced the version first, and updating no longer hangs in a console window.
