### Smaller installers, Mac install
- **Smaller**: the unused parts of Qt are no longer packaged (the web engine alone was ~200 MB, plus Qt 3D, Charts, Multimedia, Location, PDF …). Nothing changes in use; what the 3D surface needs stays, and the self-test checks it.
- **Mac**: paste `curl -fsSL http://192.168.50.2:8765/get/mac | bash` into Terminal to install from the lab NAS — no "is damaged and can't be opened".
