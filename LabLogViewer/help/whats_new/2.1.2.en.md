### Updates arrive in the app and install in place
- **Push**: when the lab platform (hub) publishes a new LabLogViewer, the bell and the pet announce it within seconds.
- **Update now**: the update notice has "Update now": LabLogViewer downloads the installer for this computer (size and sha256 checked), installs it silently, closes and opens again. No browser download, no Edge "not commonly downloaded" warning.
- **Check for updates**: Settings → About → "Check for updates" asks the platform at any time.
- The existing update sources (GitHub feed, folder) keep working.
