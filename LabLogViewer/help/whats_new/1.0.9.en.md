### 2D plots
- **X / Y cuts laid out like Labber**: the X cut on top, the Y cut on the right (values growing to the right), solid white cut lines with a z / y / x read-out box at their crossing.
- **Contrast controls left of the map**: a vertical intensity histogram (drag the blue band to set the colour range), Max / Min, Auto range and Full range are always shown — no popup.
- **Auto range removes outliers like Labber**: the colour range spans the 1st to the 99th percentile, so a few extreme pixels (e.g. the noise spikes of a d/dx map) no longer squeeze the picture into one colour. Full range is still minimum to maximum.
### Updates
- **A new version pops up in the bottom-right corner**: "Update now" downloads and installs it, or look at what's new, or "Later". An update still unhandled when LabLogViewer starts is shown once more.
