# Pen and laser pointer
> Draw lines or point with a laser on the Browser, Viewer, YIG Analysis or 3D window while presenting; nothing touches your data.

## Shortest procedure
1. **Turn on Annotation.** **Annotation** (pen icon) at the top right of the window. A glass toolbar appears at the bottom of the window. ![](annot_open.png)
2. **Choose a tool.** **Pen**: lines that stay; **Laser Pointer**: a red trail that fades. Choose a colour (red, blue, yellow, green) and **Width**; the laser has a **Trail** length. ![](annot_tools.png)
3. **Draw.** Drag with the **right** mouse button (on a trackpad, a two-finger click and drag). The left button, wheel and scroll keep panning, rotating and zooming the plot as usual. ![](annot_draw.png)
4. **Clear or finish.** **Clear Screen** removes all lines; clicking **Annotation** again closes the tools but the lines stay on the plot; to remove them later, turn Annotation on again and press **Clear Screen**.

## What the result means
- Annotation is a sheet of glass over the plot: lines stay fixed on the screen and do not follow data when you pan or zoom.
- Annotations are **never saved** and **never included in any export** (copy, save, Drag-to-Share); the lines are not stored in the file.
- While Annotation is on, plot right-click menus are paused (the right button draws) and return when it closes.

## If it does not work
- **Lines are still there after I turned Annotation off**: this is intended; turn Annotation on and press **Clear Screen**. Lines are fixed to the screen, so pan or zoom first, then draw.
- **Cannot draw on 3D**: the 3D point view is covered by its own transparent window; make sure Annotation is on and start inside the 3D plot.
- **No right-click menu**: turn Annotation off first.
