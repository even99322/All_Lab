PyGlass reference attribution
=============================

Reference: https://github.com/neomosh8/pyglass
Copyright (c) 2026 neomosh8
License: MIT

LabLogViewer v0.17D studied the reference's separation of backdrop sampling,
material/refraction processing, and foreground compositing.

The v0.17D glass rework (2026-09-25) adapts portions of pyglass/refract.py
(rounded-rectangle SDF, GlassKernel Snell refraction with per-channel
dispersion, Schlick-Fresnel environment reflection, spectral rim, box-blur
frost, and the GlassMaterial thickness/frost dials) and pyglass/effect.py
(paint_glass compositing) into app/gui/glass.py. The adapted code is ported
from PyQt6 to PySide6, retuned for toolbar-sized capsules, and modified (frost
is applied per refresh instead of per kernel). It is used under the MIT
License below, whose notice must accompany it. There is no PyQt6 or PyGlass
runtime dependency.
