### Connected to the QEL Lab platform
Active only when started from the QEL Lab launcher; on its own the app behaves as in 1.0.3.
- **Measurements open here**: when Lab Control saves a measurement, a Viewer opens for it.
- **Measure with this data's settings**: drag data from the Browser onto the Lab Control window, or choose Interfaces → Measurement; Lab Control loads that measurement's scheme (and is started if needed).
- **Related papers**: Interfaces → Online Paper Library lists the papers linked to the selected data's tags; click one to open it in the paper library.
- **Shared tags**: the lab's shared tags are added to Tags.
