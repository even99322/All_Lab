### The database refreshes itself
- New and changed measurements appear in the Browser within about **2 seconds**, without pressing Reload. The selection and the scroll position stay where they were.
- A Viewer showing a measurement that is still running follows it: new traces appear while the view (mode, traces, zoom) stays.
- Only file sizes and times are checked, and only new or changed files are read again, so it stays light, also for a database on shared storage.
- **Settings › General › Auto refresh**: Off, every 1, 2 (default) or 5 seconds.

### Updates
- After an update, this window appears once. Your records (stars, Tags, comments, Marks, sessions) are kept; when a new version needs to adapt them, a backup is made first in `update_backups` inside your data folder.
- Open this window again at any time: **Settings › About › What's New...**

### Licences
- Licence files from more than one licence issuer can be accepted, so licences keep working when the person who issues them changes.
